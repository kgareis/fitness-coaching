## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, explosive sports science aesthetic engineered for high-performance football (soccer) coaching, sprint mechanics, and physical pre-season preparation. Designed specifically for professional footballers, academy talents, and elite athletes preparing for European and international leagues, the interface projects absolute precision, velocity, and sports science authority.

The aesthetic fuses **High-Contrast Athletic Brutalism** with **Sleek Sports Data Modernism**:
- **Pitch-black and carbon substrates** emulate the intense focus of under-the-lights stadium training, cutting-edge gym conditioning facilities, and force plate telemetry hubs.
- **Electric neon lime and explosive kinetic orange** slice across the interface like tactical pitch line-markings, velocity tracking vectors, and biometric spikes.
- **Aggressive typography** leverages towering condensed headline letterforms paired with ultra-clean, technical geometric body copy, evoking dynamic scoreboard graphics and athletic diagnostics.
- The visual tone communicates elite rigor, measurable speed gains, professional club credibility (FSV Zwickau, SSV Jahn Regensburg), and no-compromise athletic excellence.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep dark mode, drawing inspiration from nocturnal football fixtures, GPS tracking heatmaps, and biometric sensors:

- **Primary (`#C6F831` - Electric Lime):** The velocity accent. Used for primary calls-to-action, active sprint indicators, high-priority metric highlights, and visual energy bursts. Its deeper variant (`#A3E635`) provides steady focus states and hover transitions.
- **Secondary (`#FF5500` - Kinetic Orange):** The explosive power tone. Directly inspired by high-intensity conditioning markers, agility cones, heart rate zone 5 spikes, and the signature REZLER badge styling. Used for booking alerts, critical conversion anchors, and secondary action highlights.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas Deep Black` (`#070A0C`): Core background canvas behind high-contrast copy.
  - `Pitch Surface` (`#0B0F12`): Base layer for primary sections and outer grid margins.
  - `Card Carbon` (`#11161B`): First-elevation cards, feature modules, and performance diagnostic panels.
  - `Card Highlight / Border` (`#1D242C` / `#2B3540`): Structural grid borders, dividers, and resting outline strokes.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - `Text Primary` (`#FFFFFF`): Maximum legibility across dense athletic data and condensed headers.
  - `Text Secondary` (`#94A3B8` / `#CBD5E1`): Supporting specs, session breakdowns, and sub-labels.
  - `Muted Border` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Subtle separating strokes between tactical modules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes instant athletic tension:

- **Headlines (`Barlow Condensed`):** Set predominantly in full uppercase with tight line heights and prominent weights (700/800). Condensed characters mirror athletic physique, speed ladders, and matchday pitch banners.
- **Body & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers technical credibility, high legibility for diagnostic training regimens, and a mechanical edge suitable for high-tech training systems (Hawkin Dynamics force plates, STATSports GPS telemetry).
- **Stylistic Treatments:**
  - Section headers, program titles, and primary stats must always appear in uppercase with subtle positive letter spacing.
  - Key numeric stats (e.g., "4 TAGE CAMP", "0.02s", "100% INDIVIDUELL") should use `Barlow Condensed` for high-impact visual punch.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a high-discipline athletic grid reflecting stadium architecture and tactical boards:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1200px+): 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, max-width 1360px centered canvas.
  - Tablet (768px - 1199px): 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 32px safe horizontal margins.
  - Mobile (under 768px): 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px edge margins.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:**
  - Content sections use generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem - 6rem on desktop) to allow high-contrast headline statements and action photography room to breathe.
  - Internal card padding remains dense and utilitarian (`space-md` to `space-lg`), reflecting technical training dossiers and performance scorecards.
  - Grid modules may span asymmetric splits (e.g., 7/5 column hero splits for action photography versus booking matrices).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Technical Stratification and Tactical Glows** instead of conventional diffuse drop shadows:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#070A0C` to `#0B0F12` pitch background, occasionally textured with subtle tactical pitch hashes or carbon mesh overlays.
- **Layer 1 (Performance Card):** `#11161B` with a continuous `1px` stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#1D242C`.
- **Layer 2 (Active Focus & Modules):** `#161D24` elevated surface with `1px` high-contrast outer stroke (`rgba(198, 248, 49, 0.4)` or `#2B3540`).
- **Kinetic Glow (State Depth):** High-priority active states (selected camp dates, main CTA hover) project an intentional neon ambient aura: `0 0 24px rgba(198, 248, 49, 0.35)` for electric lime or `0 0 24px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.35)` for performance orange.
- **Sharp Cut Dividers:** 100% width divider lines use crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` to separate coaching staff profiles, training schedule blocks, and diagnostic partner credentials.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Precision Soft-Brutalist** language (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Corner Radii:** Micro-radius corners (`0.25rem` / 4px base; `0.5rem` / 8px for major cards) deliver a disciplined, athletic silhouette. Rounding should never exceed `8px`—pill shapes are strictly avoided to prevent a soft or casual appearance.
- **Angled Accents:** Strategic components (e.g., banner tags, category badges, dynamic image masks) may incorporate subtle 45-degree chamfers or directional cutaways to reinforce kinetic sprint mechanics and forward drive.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Crisp rectangular blocks with minimal corner rounding (4px), maintaining the high-impact look of official kit badges and physical athletic equipment.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Kinetic CTA (Neon Lime):** Background `#C6F831`, text `#070A0C` (Barlow Condensed Bold, uppercase, letter-spacing +0.06em), 4px border radius. Hover introduces subtle scale (1.02) and an electric lime glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(198, 248, 49, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Impact CTA (Performance Orange):** Background `#FF5500`, text `#FFFFFF`, uppercase bold. Used exclusively for high-urgency reservations (e.g., "JETZT PLATZ SICHERN!").
- **Ghost / Technical CTA:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover changes border to `#C6F831` and text to `#C6F831`.

### Camp Date & Schedule Selectors
- **Date Matrix Badges:** Framed carbon cards (`#11161B`) with `1px` border. Displays calendar icon, condensed dates (`15.–18.06.2026`), and status indicator.
- **Selected State:** Border turns `#C6F831` with a micro neon dot (`●`) and lime accent text.

### Performance Spec Cards
- Structured data cards displaying training pillars: *Sprinttraining*, *Athletik & Kraft*, *Diagnostik (Hawkin / STATSports)*, *Regeneration & Physio*.
- Equipped with neon-outlined circular icon containers (36px × 36px) featuring high-voltage lime or orange iconography on pitch black.
- Bullet points use bright lime square or chevron glyphs instead of generic round bullets.

### Coach & Partner Credential Badges
- **Coaching Staff Cards:** High-contrast monochrome coach portrait combined with sharp uppercase credential typography (SSV Jahn Regensburg, FSV Zwickau, FCB Global Academy, DFB-Stützpunkt).
- **Partner Monochromes:** High-contrast inverted partner logos (Hawkin Dynamics, Heal Up, ENJOY Catering) placed in dedicated sponsor cells with subtle divider borders.

### Input Fields & Inquiries
- Pitch-black background (`#070A0C`), inset `1px` crisp border (`#2B3540`).
- Text in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with placeholder in `#64748B`.
- Focused state initiates a bright lime perimeter stroke (`1px solid #C6F831`) with zero blur.
